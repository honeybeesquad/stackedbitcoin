Self-Custody Explained

Many New Zealand crypto platforms hold your Bitcoin on your behalf — they store the private keys, and you trust them not to freeze, lose, or misappropriate your funds. That's custodial.

Self-custody means you hold the private keys. Stacked's wallet generates your keys locally on your device and encrypts them with your PIN or biometrics. Your recovery phrase (12 words) is the backup — if you lose your phone, you can restore your entire wallet on any compatible app.

With self-custody:

  • No exchange can freeze your account
  • No third party can lose your funds through poor security or bad management
  • Your Bitcoin is accessible anywhere, anytime, as long as you have your recovery phrase

The tradeoff is personal responsibility — if you lose your recovery phrase, no one can recover your funds. That's why secure backup matters.

Security Tips for New Zealanders

  • Write down your recovery phrase offline — not in Notes, not in cloud storage. A metal backup is even better for long-term holders.
  • Never share your private keys or recovery phrase — Stacked will never ask for them.
  • Enable biometrics — use Face ID or fingerprint as a second layer of protection on top of your PIN.
  • Verify receiving addresses — check the first and last few characters of an address before sending large amounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Stacked wallet available in New Zealand?

Yes. The Stacked wallet is built for New Zealanders — NZD deposits and withdrawals, local bank support, and English-language support from a New Zealand team.

Can I use Lightning to pay New Zealand merchants?

Yes. Lightning is designed for everyday spending — you can pay anyone with a Lightning address or invoice, including participating NZ businesses.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your Bitcoin is safe. Using your 12-word recovery phrase, you can restore your entire wallet on any compatible Bitcoin app. Keep the phrase somewhere secure and private.

Does Stacked hold my keys?

No. The Stacked wallet is self-custodial — keys are generated and stored on your device. Stacked has no access to your funds.

Can I stack sats automatically?

Yes. Combine the Stacked wallet with the Stacked exchange for automatic recurring purchases — set a weekly or fortnightly schedule and your Bitcoin buys itself automatically.
