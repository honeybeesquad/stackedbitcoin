What is a Bitcoin Savings Plan?

A Bitcoin savings plan is a strategy where you accumulate Bitcoin on a regular schedule — weekly or monthly — regardless of the price. This is called Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA), and it's one of the most recommended approaches for long-term Bitcoin holders.

Instead of trying to time the market, you buy a fixed amount of Bitcoin at regular intervals. Over time, this smooths out volatility and means you don't need to watch the charts or make emotional decisions.

Why New Zealanders Are Starting Bitcoin Savings Plans

  • Hedge against NZ dollar devaluation — the New Zealand dollar has lost purchasing power over decades. Bitcoin's fixed supply makes it a different kind of savings vehicle.
  • Automated, hands-off approach — set up a plan once and let it run. No need to check prices or make decisions every week.
  • Small regular amounts — you don't need a lump sum. Start with $10 or $50 per week and build over time.
  • Stacked's automatic stacking — schedule weekly or monthly Bitcoin purchases on a NZ platform designed specifically for Bitcoin savers, not traders. See the Bitcoin-only alternative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Bitcoin savings plan a good idea?

For long-term savers who believe in Bitcoin's value proposition, a DCA savings plan is one of the most consistently recommended strategies. It removes emotion from the equation and builds your position over time regardless of price.

How much should I save in Bitcoin each week?

That depends on your financial situation. Many New Zealanders start with $10–$50 per week. The key is consistency — small regular amounts add up significantly over years.

What happens if the Bitcoin price drops?

With DCA, a lower price means your regular amount buys more Bitcoin. This is actually the benefit of the strategy — you automatically accumulate more when prices are low, averaging out your cost over time.

Can I withdraw my Bitcoin from the savings plan?

Yes. Your Bitcoin is in your account and can be withdrawn to any wallet you control at any time. Stacked doesn't lock your funds.

Is Stacked safe for savings?

Stacked is FSP-registered and designed specifically for New Zealanders. For large savings, many users withdraw to their own hardware wallet for maximum security — while still using Stacked's automatic stacking to accumulate.
