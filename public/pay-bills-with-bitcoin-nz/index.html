Stacked Bill Pay: Any NZ Bill, Paid With Bitcoin

Stacked Bill Pay lets you pay any New Zealand bill from your self-custody wallet. Enter the biller's details, and Stacked converts your Bitcoin to NZD and sends it directly via bank transfer. The biller receives NZD — they never need to accept crypto or even know you paid with Bitcoin.

No third-party services. No finding a biller who "accepts Bitcoin." No manual steps. Just your wallet, your Bitcoin, your bills — paid.

How It Works

  1. Add a biller — enter their name, bank account number, and the amount. Save them for next time.
  2. Choose when to pay — pay now, when the bill is due. You're in control.
  3. Stacked converts and sends — the Bitcoin equivalent is debited from your wallet at the current rate, converted to NZD, and sent to the biller.
  4. Done — the biller gets NZD in their bank account. You kept your Bitcoin until the moment of payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay any NZ bill with Stacked?

Yes. Stacked Bill Pay works with any New Zealand biller that accepts bank transfers — power companies, internet providers, landlords, phone plans, insurance, and more. You hold Bitcoin, Stacked converts the exact amount to NZD and pays the biller on your behalf.

How does Stacked Bill Pay work?

You enter the biller's details in your Stacked wallet — account number, amount, and due date. Stacked debits the Bitcoin equivalent from your wallet at the current exchange rate and sends NZD directly to the biller via bank transfer. It's that simple.

What fees does Stacked charge for bill payments?

Stacked charges the standard 1% conversion fee on the Bitcoin-to-NZD exchange, plus any applicable bank transfer fee. There are no additional bill pay markups or subscription fees.

Does the biller need to accept Bitcoin?

No. The biller receives NZD via standard bank transfer — they don't need to know or care that you paid with Bitcoin. Stacked handles the conversion behind the scenes.

Can I save regular billers in Stacked?

Yes. Stacked Bill Pay is designed so you can save biller details and reuse them for future payments. That makes recurring NZ bills like rent, power, internet, and phone payments easier to pay from your Bitcoin balance.
